      How the rating works

      Every hour gets five scores from 0 to 1: size (how close the waves are to your board's sweet spot), wind (light offshore is perfect, onshore blows it out), period (longer period means cleaner, more powerful waves), tide (), and direction (how open the break is to the swell). They are multiplied together, with size counting most, then scaled to 10 on a curve. A 10 means every factor is perfect at once, which is rare. A normal fun day lands around 5 to 7.

      Wave height at the beach is estimated from the offshore swell, adjusted for period (longer swells grow more as they reach shore) and for direction (how open the break is to that swell). Beach breaks like Pacific Beach Drive pick up a little more size than Tourmaline. When the Torrey Pines buoy is reachable, the next day of the forecast is corrected toward what the buoy is actually measuring.
