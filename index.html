How the rating works

Every hour gets four scores from 0 to 1: size (is the wave height in your board's sweet spot), wind (offshore and glassy is best, onshore blows it out), period (longer period means cleaner, more powerful waves), and tide (Tourmaline's reef and rocks like mid to high tide). They are multiplied together, with size counting most, then scaled to 10.

Wave height at the beach is estimated from the offshore swell, adjusted for period (longer swells grow more as they reach shore) and for direction (how open Tourmaline is to that swell). When the Torrey Pines buoy is reachable, the next day of the forecast is corrected toward what the buoy is actually measuring.
